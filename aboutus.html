About Us

Company Information

  • Name: ZEN TIME
  • Established: November 16, 2024
  • Location: Dharan 18
  • Description: Our company specializes in selling excellent quality watches at reasonable prices. We are one of Nepal’s top watch retailers with international partnerships. Our products are crafted in our own business unit using locally sourced materials, supporting Nepali farmers. Our main focus is selling watches to young teenagers and adults.
  • Products: Rolex, Casio, Patek Philippe, Gurkha, Apple

Mission and Vision

  • Mission: To offer a vast selection of high-quality, tasteful watches at affordable prices. We aim to provide a seamless online shopping experience with excellent customer service, authentication, and craftsmanship.
  • Vision: To become the leading online platform for elegant and innovative watch designs, offering customers a sophisticated and luxurious shopping experience. Our goal is to cultivate a growing community passionate about horology and watch trends.
