How Technology can Boost Watch Sales.

Technology has been a complete paradigm shift for the watch industry, revolutionizing product features and customer experiences using increased
reach to drive sales. Smartwatches, with their fitness tracking, heart rate monitoring, GPS connectivity, and app integration, have attracted the
tech-savvy consumer who enjoys having a multifunctional device. Advancements such as cutting CNC machining and 3D printing trickle down into
the traditional watch, allowing the use of intricate designs and offering higher-quality materials such as titanium and carbon fiber. With e-commerce
coming to the fore, watches became general commodities, portraying their prowess in marketing. Online platforms with AR/VR-enabled virtual
testing allow consumers to attempt watches on their wrists, thereby decreasing the flinch of purchasing. Customization in the watch face, strap,
and AI recommendations makes for an individualized shopping experience, tripling customer engagement. Read More
